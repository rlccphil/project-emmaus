# Vision

Our desire is not to automate ministry.

Our desire is to remove unnecessary administrative friction so that shepherds can spend more time loving people.

Jesus did not merely organize disciples.

He walked with them.

He listened.

He prayed.

He encouraged.

He corrected.

He sent them.

Emmaus exists to help pastors imitate that pattern.

Every feature should answer one question:

"Does this help a shepherd know, pray for, care for, or disciple someone more faithfully?"
